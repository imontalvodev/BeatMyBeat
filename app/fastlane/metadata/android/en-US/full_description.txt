BeatMyBeat is a free, open-source Android music client. Play your local library, organize playlists and favorites, and download audio from YouTube or YouTube Music — without ads or a proprietary backend.

<b>Features</b>
• Local playback with queue, shuffle, and repeat
• Scan music on device (MediaStore and custom folders)
• Search and download from YouTube / YouTube Music
• Multiple audio formats (MP3, M4A, FLAC, …) via FFmpeg
• Synced lyrics (LRCLIB) with offline cache and batch download
• Custom themes and multiple languages

<b>Privacy</b>
No analytics, no accounts, no BeatMyBeat servers. Network access is used only for features you trigger (YouTube, lyrics APIs). See PRIVACY.md in the source repository.

<b>Responsible use</b>
You are responsible for complying with copyright and the terms of content platforms. Download only content you have the right to use.
